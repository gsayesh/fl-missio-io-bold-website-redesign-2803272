Giant type and confident colour blocks bring the specialist-agency boldness; real product UI, real customers, cited sources and an honest comparison bring the trust. Brand teal, amber and paper are kept — just pushed harder.

Plain-spoken“One login”, not “a unified solution”. Words a board member would repeat.
SpecificHours, days, line items, named people. A number beats an adjective.
WarmName the humans — donors, volunteers, staff. The software is never the hero.
Confident, never hypeSay what others leave out — then cite it. No “revolutionary”.

Interaction notes
  • Mega-menu: click or 150 ms hover-intent; Esc closes; focus trapped.
  • Sub-nav: sticks after the hero; “Let’s talk” always visible.
  • Motion: 200–320 ms ease-out; honours reduced-motion.
